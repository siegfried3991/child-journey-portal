成長志 / Child Journey — research-led product design

Prepared 20 October 2026. This is a fresh design for the expanded brief, with a working synthetic demonstration. The previous starter pack remains intact. The 50 proposed Chinese abilities have been retained as editable product vocabulary; they are not official game attributes or a psychometric instrument.

Decision in one paragraph

Build a private, mobile-first learning and life-planning portal with a light game-like presentation. The home page should answer: What is happening now? What is changing? What does the student want to explore? What is the next useful step? Use ILO ISCO-08 as the occupation reference, Hong Kong QF/SCS/SGC as qualification and competency reference material, and dated schoolwork, projects, feedback and student reflections as the actual evidence. Use ordinary web components and original SVG scenes first. Keep several possible futures visible and revise plans together when interests, circumstances or results change.

What “based on ILO and 香港資歷評審” means

There is no single official framework that measures a child's whole development and maps it mechanically to a career. The product therefore needs separate layers:

Layer Official or proposed? It answers It must not imply
ILO ISCO-08 occupations Official reference What types of work exist, and what tasks do they involve? A career-fit score, intelligence ranking or complete industry list
HKQF and qualifications register Official reference What recognised qualification is this, at which level, for which period? That a parent or this app can award a qualification
SCS / SGC / vocational pathways Official reference Which work-related competencies and progressions are described? That school activities satisfy professional assessment requirements

ISCO-08 contains 10 major groups, 43 sub-major groups, 130 minor groups and 436 unit groups; a unit group can contain numerous job titles. Its skill levels describe occupational requirements, not the child's personal level. The published revision timetable targets discussion of a near-final draft in 2028. Continue using an explicit ISCO-08 version until a successor is actually adopted and migrated. ILO overview, revision programme.

The Hong Kong qualifications system has seven levels. Its GLD domains are 知識及智能；過程；自主性及問責性；溝通、資訊及通訊科技及運算. These provide a useful organisation of evidence, but official qualification recognition and daily learning observations need different records and visual labels. Hong Kong QF levels.

CASEL identifies self-awareness, self-management, social awareness, relationship skills and responsible decision-making as interconnected areas of social-emotional learning. These can inform the questions the portal asks; the proposed interface is not a validated CASEL assessment. CASEL framework.

Hong Kong EDB describes life planning as ongoing self-understanding, planning, reflection and revision connected to whole-person development. Its values-education guidance supports contextual discussion and judgement. The portal should let a family or school select relevant values and retain their source, rather than treating all cultural, civic or political attitudes as measurable attributes. EDB life planning, EDB values education.

Screen design

1. 成長總覽 — the home screen

Use an original classroom/home/community illustration, with an age-appropriate avatar and a configurable school emblem on the right. The demonstration uses a fictional 15-year-old and a fictional emblem. A production scene follows a timetable or manual setting; it is not proof of the student's physical location. On small screens the scene becomes a short banner and essential records remain ordinary selectable text.

The first screen contains: current chapter or exploration theme; a short quotation written by the student; imminent tasks; the latest actionable feedback; evidence coverage across domains; the next low-cost experience; and a link to compare future routes. “12 of 50 skills have evidence” is a coverage indicator, not “24% of a complete person”. No radar chart is used to imply that incomparable qualities share a measurement scale.

2. 學習記錄 — from individual homework to patterns

Record homework, tests and examinations independently. Preserve absent, exempt, ungraded, late, missing, submitted and graded as distinct states. Keep raw numerator/denominator or school grade; a B+ and 34/40 should not silently become a blended average. Teachers' weighting and conversion rules belong to a specific course, not a universal setting. Resits and corrections are linked attempts, not extra independent tests.

Clicking an item opens original evidence, teacher feedback, linked learning objectives, the student's reflection, support received and the next action. Charts compare explicitly comparable series and show sample size and assessment dates. A changed syllabus, grading scale or assessment difficulty should break or annotate a trend.

3. 能力圖鑑 — 50 Chinese abilities

Group abilities into eight manageable areas; show all 50 on demand, not all on the homepage. An observation captures task, date, context, observer, student response, support and limitations. Proposed descriptions such as “with guidance”, “independent in this task” and “transferred to a new situation” remain separate from QF levels. Missing evidence is not low ability.

The existing original list includes reading/writing/listening, reasoning/experimentation, programming/making, art/design, planning/reflection, empathy/cooperation, daily-life skills and movement. The detailed bilingual catalogue accompanies the project as JSON and in this report. The labels may be renamed with the child; no ability should become a fixed identity.

4. 職業探索 — the work world as discoverable territories

Provide a complete official major-group index and an editorial activity-led navigation. The demonstration has 15 exploration families with selected unit-code examples; it is not the complete ISCO catalogue. Occupations can appear in several families. Keep industry, sector, occupation, work setting and skills as separate tags; a hospital employs many kinds of workers.

Every territory should offer a low-cost home/school activity, a practitioner conversation, a visit or workshop type, an age-adjusted project and a reflection prompt. Public-service and armed-forces occupation references can be explained in the full index without making weapons or combat activities part of child exploration. Paid activities should not receive more status than accessible school/community alternatives. Show costs, access needs, travel, language, adult supervision and eligibility before suggesting a real event.

Research supports repeated exploration, experiences of work and conversations about futures. The OECD evidence is largely associative and cannot establish that any particular activity will cause success for this child. OECD career guidance.

5. 未來路線 — several futures, visible conditions

Start with the student's statement: “I want to make useful things”, “I care about animals”, “I enjoy explaining”, or “I am still exploring”. Translate it into two or three candidate routes with a shared near-term experiment, not a prematurely fixed job.

Show each route as: now → subject/experience choices → programme/training → subsequent options. Each link has conditions, source, intake year, review date, cost/time considerations and uncertainty. Display academic, applied/vocational, apprenticeship and exploratory routes without status ranking. Course entry, credit transfer, professional recognition and actual offers are different milestones.

When new results or circumstances arrive, generate a proposed change with its reason and alternatives. The student/family review it, retain the old version, and can reverse the decision. A low result should first prompt attention to learning needs, context and support; it should never silently erase an aspiration. See the evidence-and-pathways specification for the exact rules.

6. 家庭回顧 / portfolio — production feature

A weekly five-minute view: one accomplishment, one difficulty, one request for support and one next step. A term review: compare work samples and revisit route assumptions. Student-authored reflections stay visibly distinct from teacher/parent observations. Formal sharing is an intentional, scoped export with expiry and preview; grades and private notes are absent from a default WeChat share card.

Gentle game mechanics

Borrow the feeling of chapters, an explorable map, quests, a journal and visible completed projects. Do not reuse commercial assets or make achievements depend on relentless daily streaks. The demo deliberately has no numerical “total level”. A future reward can acknowledge completing a reflection or trying something new, with no inference that more clicks or hours equal skill. No public leaderboard, paid-experience multiplier, punishment for rest, or permanent label from a bad week.

Personalisation assumptions and open inputs

The demonstration is Traditional Chinese and uses invented secondary-school records. It does not identify the user's child, curriculum or school. The first real configuration needs age/year, curriculum, current subjects, reporting scales, student interests, target regions, languages, learning/access supports, household time/budget, who can see what and whether the app is for one family or a school. Residency/application category must be verified for admissions routes, not inferred from school location.

Prototype decisions do not require those answers. Personalised admission routes and real-data deployment do.

Definition of success

A parent can understand the present situation within one minute; a student can see their own voice and choices; every suggested action has an explanation; every purported qualification or admission rule has a source; and the family can change direction without losing history. Launch success is demonstrated with observed usability and secure record handling, not the number of badges issued.

Research decisions and important boundaries

Checked 20 October 2026. Detailed source notes are in research/ilo.md, research/hk-framework.md and research/platform.md.

Findings that materially change the design

  1. ILO is a work classification, not a developmental rubric. Keep official codes for a reference index and use a separate child-friendly exploration taxonomy. A code can represent a cluster of jobs. Do not claim the demonstration includes all jobs.
  2. 香港資歷評審 is not a single set of child traits. HKQF, HKCAAVQ, QR, SCS, SGC, VQP, CAT and RPL have different purposes. Use exact terms. A school activity may relate to a competency theme without satisfying professional assessment conditions.
  3. Aspirations should change without being erased. Store interest, exposure, demonstrated learning, application requirements and earned qualifications independently. Draft route changes from evidence and obtain human review before adopting them.
  4. A web portal fits the task better than an engine-first game. Most interactions are forms, filters, tables, evidence and pathway comparisons. SVG is enough for the requested classroom illustration. Phaser is a free optional rendering layer if richer gameplay later earns its complexity. Godot remains technically possible but adds web-export constraints without solving the core problem.
  5. GitHub and WeChat address different problems. GitHub can version code and research. A deployed authenticated app handles private student data. An H5 page can be designed for WeChat's browser, but real-device testing and account/domain checks are separate work. A Mini Program is an additional product, not a checkbox.
  6. Public guidance is not an individual admissions decision. Requirements belong to a programme, intake and applicant category. Unknown evidence remains unknown. Minimum requirements do not predict offers. AP/IB/A-level/HKDSE scales must not be converted with an invented universal formula.

Scope of this completed research

The investigation reviewed official ILO taxonomy and revision information; Hong Kong QF/GLD, assessment authority, register, competency specifications and pathway material; education-route examples; whole-person and career-guidance references; free-engine and modern web documentation; GitHub hosting boundaries; Tencent/Weixin integration issues; and relevant mainland/Hong Kong privacy guidance.

It produced a working sample portal, a product design, a proposed 50-ability catalogue, career-exploration examples, an evidence/pathway specification and a phased implementation plan. This is comprehensive product research within those boundaries, not a review of every occupation, every university, every national school system or every legal regime.

Evidence limitations to carry into development

  • No actual student record, curriculum, account, grade or interest was inspected. No school-platform integration exists.
  • The full ISCO machine-readable corpus was not successfully retrieved by the research tools. Code examples are a reviewed seed; dataset rights and completeness remain ingestion work.
  • Official Weixin developer pages were not retrievable in this run. Tencent documentation supports domain setup, but precise account entitlements and review conditions require verification in the eventual account.
  • No physical iPhone/Android WeChat or mainland-network test has occurred. A mobile browser viewport is a design check only.
  • No institution/programme was selected for a real application. Some linked examples concern a past or current intake; each source's applicable year must remain visible.
  • No recognised qualification was verified for the student. QF-themed evidence tags are interpretations, not endorsements or equivalence determinations.
  • Legal applicability depends on personal/family use versus service operation, geography and data flows. The research identifies boundaries and launch questions; it does not pronounce a universal compliance result.
  • Technology choices are an engineering assessment. No head-to-head game-engine performance benchmark was run.

Source handling

Keep URL, document title, issuing body, edition/publication date where known, accessed date, applicable intake, rights status and claim supported. Separate a document's revision date from the date it was retrieved. Mark a cached source stale when its intake changes. Keep official data separate from editorial mappings and record rationale/reviewer on each mapping. Do not republish entire third-party corpora until item-specific reuse terms are confirmed.

What to build first

Start with a single-family private pilot: manually verified schoolwork, evidence attachments, student reflections, a small curated occupation set and two or three versioned routes. Make export, deletion, restore and household permissions work before integrations. Validate the experience with the student as well as the parent. Expand the occupation catalogue and detailed programme paths only as their sources and mappings are reviewed.

Evidence, abilities and pathway decisions

Status: implementation specification, version 1.0. Research checked 20 October 2026. Companion research: research/hk-framework.md. Ability catalogue: data/abilities.json, catalogue version 1, exactly 50 original portal abilities.

1. Product boundary

The portal connects what a student has done, what the evidence supports, what the student wants to explore and what a published route requires. It does not diagnose a child, certify professional competence, award QF credits or predict admission chances.

Keep school results, skill observations, student interests, route requirements and earned qualifications separate. A completed homework can contribute evidence to several abilities, but its grade must never automatically increase a personality, ethics or QF score. The overview presents a short evidence-backed narrative and next actions, with the underlying records one tap away.

Official reference boundaries:

  • HKQF's four GLD domains describe qualification learning outcomes. Using their themes for school observations is our product adaptation. HKQF domains.
  • SCS/SGC units and VQP are useful reference material for work-related learning and routes; a local task is not an assessment of the complete professional unit. HKQF applications.
  • QF credits measure notional learning volume, rather than elapsed activity time. QF credit.
  • Admission and credit transfer are distinct institutional decisions. CAT.

3. Grades and comparability

Original results

result_status is one of not_yet_assessed, pending, graded, absent, exempt, missing_submission, withdrawn, invalidated. A school may explicitly award zero for missing work; record the school's zero plus the relevant status/policy. The portal must not invent that zero.

For numeric marks, keep raw_value, maximum, minimum, unit, precision and scale_id. For categorical grades, keep original_label and the official ordered category ID. HKDSE 5, 5*, 5**, IB subject grades and AP grades are distinct categories/scales. Predicted grades carry is_predicted=true, predicting source and date, and must never satisfy a requirement for an actual award unless that requirement explicitly accepts predictions for that stage.

Valid comparisons

An assessment belongs to a comparison_series_id only after a teacher or authorised adult confirms that it measures sufficiently comparable objectives with the same scale and appropriately comparable difficulty/context. Store that decision and rationale. Different accommodations or access arrangements do not automatically invalidate a comparison; obtain context-sensitive review rather than penalising support.

Situation Permitted display/calculation
Same assessment, corrected submission Side-by-side attempts; labelled improvement on that task
Comparable assessment series Trend within that series, sample count, dates and method
Same numeric bounds, different content/difficulty Original marks side-by-side; no asserted mastery trend merely because both are percentages
Different subjects/curricula Separate panels; no combined “intelligence” or universal grade

Missing weights or uncertain policy Show provisional individual results and “aggregate unavailable”

For a valid weighted numeric aggregation, use sum(weight × normalized_score) / sum(included_weight), where normalization is authorised by the scale/policy and the denominator follows the school's missing-work policy. For a scale explicitly defined as points from zero to maximum, normalized_score = raw / maximum. Otherwise use the scale's declared transformation, never guess. Report coverage, e.g. “3 of 4 assessments; remaining result pending”. Do not silently renormalise pending weight unless the school policy specifies it. Keep full precision until the policy's rounding step.

A chart may display normalized percentages for inspection only if it states the original marks and that percentage alone does not establish comparable difficulty. Avoid green/red career judgements from one low test.

4. Local observations and whole-child development

Local stages are trying_with_support (在協助下嘗試), following_guidance (按指引完成), independent_in_context (在此情境能獨立完成), and transfer_and_explain (能在新情境運用及解釋). not_assessed (未有足夠證據) is a separate missing state, not stage zero. These are not age norms or HKQF levels.

Every reviewed stage must link to observable behaviour, a defined task and support context. An introductory task cannot establish advanced competence in the whole field. The default summary shows the latest reviewed, undisputed observation in context; broader claims require evidence across contexts and a reviewer explanation. Newer contradictory evidence shows “情境有差異，待討論”, rather than choosing whichever result looks better. No automatic age-based decay or demotion.

Whole-child views include learning, relationships/belonging, responsibility/ethical reasoning, creative/practical exploration, autonomy and wellbeing. Wellbeing is a voluntary check-in and support conversation. Ethical reflection records a situation, affected people, choices, reasons, consequences, repair and next action. Examples: crediting sources; admitting an error; asking permission; respecting a refusal; sharing group work fairly. Never assign “morality 78/100”, infer dishonesty from a low mark, grade family beliefs, or create a compliance leaderboard. Social skill does not mean popularity or extroversion. Disability, neurodiversity, language learning and contextual support must not be converted into character deficits.

Applicability first

Before checking grades, match jurisdiction, programme, intake/round, curriculum route and applicant category. A version intended for HKDSE local applicants cannot evaluate an AP non-local application. If context is missing or ambiguous, return unknown and explain the missing classification. An inapplicable version is excluded from evaluation, not recorded as a failed child requirement. When no applicable verified version remains, the route is unknown.

JUPAS eligibility depends on results and identity conditions; it cannot be inferred solely from school location. JUPAS. Institution-specific standards may differ between IB, A-level and SAT/AP and by intake; meeting a published threshold is not an admission guarantee. HKU international admissions.

Deterministic logic

Each predicate returns a value plus reason_code, input IDs, source locator and any unresolved data. Required evidence absent from the portal is unknown, not failure. A transcript proving a grade below the published minimum is not_demonstrated. A confirmed not-yet-earned prerequisite is not_demonstrated for a current-readiness check, with “not yet earned” rather than “cannot achieve”. A predicted result is unknown when the rule requires final results. Disputed, invalidated or unreviewed extraction cannot satisfy a predicate.

  • all: any not_demonstrated ⇒ not_demonstrated; otherwise any unknown ⇒ unknown; otherwise eligible.
  • any: any eligible ⇒ eligible; otherwise any unknown ⇒ unknown; otherwise not_demonstrated.
  • at_least(n): if eligible count ≥ n, eligible; if eligible + unknown count < n, not_demonstrated; otherwise unknown.
  • A source-level gate runs before the expression: incomplete scope, stale/changed source awaiting review, unresolved applicability or conflicting authoritative rules ⇒ overall unknown. Preserve individual known shortfalls as findings even when the overall result is unknown.
  • A closed application round is a separate application-readiness predicate; it does not erase an academic qualification or change its grade.
  • Round numeric values only according to the cited rule. Equality at a threshold passes when the rule says “at least”. Do not add tolerances without an explicit rule.
  • Do not infer probability, rank, “safety school”, or a predicted future result from this logic.

Each evaluation is reproducible from immutable requirement, input and engine versions. Human reviewers may correct data or clarify a rule through a new version, but cannot flip the result without recording the new evidence/rule. A scenario with hypothetical grades uses a distinct scenario_id, visible “假設情境” styling and separate storage; it never modifies actual records.

7. Human-reviewed route revision

Trigger a draft review when a final result changes, a new comparable assessment identifies a support need, a child changes interests, a subject changes, a requirement/source changes, a deadline approaches/passes, or the family changes a planning constraint. One low homework score alone produces a learning-support suggestion, not an automatic career exclusion.

Processing sequence:

  1. Capture an immutable input snapshot and current adopted plan version.
  2. Re-evaluate only the affected, applicable requirement sets; mark stale or missing sources unknown.
  3. Generate a deterministic change set: new evidence, changed predicates, missing facts, expired conditions, completed transferable learning and affected actions. Stable-sort by route ID and predicate ID so equal inputs produce equal output.
  4. Suggest actions using explicit rules. Examples: missing final language evidence → obtain result or ask the institution about alternatives; below a published subject requirement → review accepted retake/bridging routes; reduced interest → offer a different trial rather than an attainment penalty.
  5. Display the existing and proposed route side-by-side, including rationale, source, time/cost changes and reversibility. Do not hide an aspirational route merely because requirements are not yet met.
  6. Parent/counsellor reviews source interpretation; student discusses the direction. The plan adopts only after the authorised family member confirms the exact draft revision and records the child's agreement, disagreement or preference to defer. This is a product workflow, not an external consent assertion.
  7. Save a new adopted version, retain the prior plan and support rollback. Reject adoption if inputs changed since the draft; re-evaluate before review.

Every pathway edge has a claim type: illustrative, published_possibility, formal_articulation_agreement, or confirmed_individual_decision. An agreement needs the applicable intake, subject match, grade/credit conditions and source. A published route remains conditional until the receiving institution makes the relevant decision. Credit transfer and admission remain separate. CAT.

9. Acceptance criteria

Evidence and grades

  1. Importing 17/25 preserves 17, 25, the original rubric and the source; zero, missing, exempt and pending display differently.
  2. A missing result never becomes zero unless a recorded school policy explicitly supplies that grade.
  3. Two assessments with equal denominators cannot automatically join a comparison series. A reviewer action and rationale are required.
  4. A retake/correction preserves both attempts and uses only the attempt specified by the versioned school/admission policy.
  5. AP, IB and HKDSE scales remain distinct; categorical labels such as 5** survive round-trip import/export unchanged.
  6. A weighted aggregate with unknown policy or weights is unavailable; a valid aggregate shows policy, rounding, included records and coverage.
  7. Clicking a skill summary opens the observations, exact evidence locators, support context and author. No stage appears without reviewed evidence.
  8. Disputed observations remain visible to authorised reviewers but cannot silently establish a qualification/entry predicate. A withdrawal recalculates derived views.
  9. All 50 catalogue IDs are represented exactly once in the mapping table. No mapping stores an awarded QF level. Unmapped-core abilities remain usable.
  10. No screen/API field provides an overall child score, ethics score, popularity score or psychological diagnosis. Interest and attainment can disagree without either being overwritten.

Requirements and pathway changes

  1. A local-HKDSE requirement version does not evaluate an international/non-local application. Missing applicability returns unknown with a reason.
  2. Required original/actual evidence cannot be satisfied by a prediction, simulation, unverified attachment or unreviewed extraction unless the rule explicitly permits that type and stage.
  3. AND/OR/at-least truth tables pass all three-state combinations; exact-threshold, duplicate-result, retake and multi-sitting fixtures follow the declared rule.
  4. Partial, changed or stale requirements prevent overall eligible status. The UI exposes stale-source date and known shortfalls without asserting final ineligibility.
  5. Identical inputs, requirement versions and engine version produce identical results and explanation IDs; changes create a new evaluation rather than mutating the old one.
  6. Academic eligibility, application readiness, credit transfer, offer and enrolment remain distinct. Expired application rounds do not invalidate earned grades or qualifications.
  7. Every explanation links both the rule's authoritative source and the student's relevant evidence, with access controls enforced separately.
  8. A draft route never becomes adopted automatically. Adoption checks the base-plan/input revision and fails cleanly when outdated.
  9. A family can retain an aspiration despite a presently unmet requirement; alternatives show required steps and uncertainty without promised admission.
  10. Scenario grades never enter actual transcripts, observations or awarded-qualification records. Scenario exports are visibly marked hypothetical.
  11. Every adopted route has version history, reason, reviewer, child's recorded perspective and rollback. A deleted personal record is removed from derived views and retained snapshots according to the retention policy.
  12. Student, sibling and family boundaries are enforced by the storage/API layer, including attachments and exports; hiding a panel is not authorisation.

10. MVP implementation order

First ship manual/CSV assessment entry with original scales, evidence uploads with review, the 50-ability catalogue, voluntary reflections and two or three family-authored aspirations. Add requirements through a small reviewed catalogue, three-state checks and versioned route drafts. Automated extraction, institution integrations and large-scale occupation-to-competency crosswalks follow only after provenance, permissions, correction and evaluation tests work. Source refresh is a review queue; it must not silently replace active rules.

4. Getting reliable records into the portal

Start with manual entry and an agreed CSV template. Record the original grade, maximum/scale, assessment type, date, course, teacher/source, weighting if known, and whether a mark is provisional, final or corrected. Missing is different from zero; submitted is different from marked. Never combine different exam systems or incomparable assessments into a misleading average.

The import workflow is:

  1. Select student and source; upload only the intended document or file.
  2. Parse into a preview with original rows/pages and suspected duplicates.
  3. Show each proposed record, extracted field and ambiguity; nothing is yet authoritative.
  4. An authorised adult confirms or corrects the proposed entries.
  5. Commit a validated batch with import ID, source reference, reviewer and timestamps.
  6. Report added, updated, skipped and rejected items; allow correction or reversal without erasing provenance.

A file hash plus source identifiers and assessment context can help find duplicates, but should not replace user review where source identifiers are absent. Re-importing an unchanged batch must not create duplicate assignments, marks, activities or progress credit. Corrected marks need revision history and a visible reason.

PDF/image extraction is a later assisted step, not a promise of accurate OCR. Retain the source page/region; mark uncertain fields, unsupported scales and unreadable content. Redact unnecessary identifiers. If an external OCR or AI service is considered, review the data flow and provider terms before actual records are sent. Require human verification of marks and deadlines regardless of model confidence.

Cialfo, school portals, Teams or other systems require documented access and a supported integration route. No Cialfo API entitlement or school-system integration has been verified for this project. A signed-in browser page does not establish API access or permission to bulk extract student records. Use user-provided exports first; do not store school passwords in the application.

Research checked: 20 October 2026. All examples concern a fictional learner. Proposed Chinese labels, exploration families, mappings, activities and product rules below are editorial design, not an ILO standard, assessment or endorsement.

1. What the portal should take from ILO

Use ISCO-08 as the versioned reference for occupations. Its hierarchy contains 10 major groups, 43 sub-major groups, 130 minor groups and 436 unit groups, represented by one to four digits. A unit group can include several occupation titles; 436 is therefore not a count of every individual job title. ISCO classifies work by tasks and duties, with skill level and specialization helping organize the structure. It does not assess an individual child's character, interests, intelligence or future success. ILO ISCO-08 overview

The official revision page still describes work in progress: a near-final draft is scheduled for discussion at the 2028 International Conference of Labour Statisticians, with availability intended for the 2030 census round. This is a published timetable, not proof of future adoption. I found no adopted replacement in the official sources checked. Keep the data model ready for a new version rather than inventing “ISCO-2026”. ILO revision notice

Occupation and industry are separate dimensions. Occupation concerns what a person does; industry concerns the economic activity of the establishment. A software developer can work in a hospital, bank, school or games studio. Store occupation codes separately from optional industry codes; the child-facing exploration categories below deliberately cut across both. ILOSTAT on economic activities

4. Age adaptation and fair access

Use the learner's actual maturity, access needs, consent and school arrangements to adjust these indicative age bands. They are product defaults, not developmental norms.

Stage Suitable form Typical output
About 6–9 Play, short demonstrations, family interviews, supervised making, local visits A drawing, photo of work, voice reflection or one sentence.
About 10–13 Small projects, clubs, structured visits, practitioner talks, teamwork with rotating roles A small artifact, process notes and a short “try again / curious / not for now” reflection.
About 14–17 Longer projects, practitioner interviews, supervised job shadowing, permitted volunteering and work experience Portfolio evidence, task/working-condition reflection, questions about qualification routes, next experiment.

Every family should include a free/home-based option and a low-cost community option. Lack of opportunity is not lack of interest or ability. Do not use expenditure, prestigious provider names or number of paid camps as a progress score. Offer simulation or remote practitioner discussion when a workplace visit is unavailable. For health, construction, machinery and sensitive services, use safe demonstrations or supervised observation instead of exposing the child to professional hazards or confidential records.

ILO career-development materials include career information, mentoring and work exposure as parts of a broader support system. OECD research links teenagers' exploration, workplace exposure and career conversations with later outcomes; these are associations, not individual predictions. The OECD job-shadowing guide commonly discusses ages 13–16, so use formal school/provider safeguards for such visits rather than making them a default for young children. 5. How this becomes a progress portal

Keep exposure, interest, demonstrated performance and future plans separate. A child can be interested but inexperienced, competent but uninterested, or undecided. A suggested record contains:

  • Family and optional occupation links; activity date and form; opportunity/access context.
  • Child's interest before and after: curious / enjoyed parts / want to repeat / not for now / unsure; no forced answer.
  • A concrete artifact or observation, with author and visibility permissions.
  • Two or three linked capabilities, each judged against an explicit task rubric; no automatic capability increase because an activity was attended.
  • Support needed, degree of independence, feedback and next action.
  • Child reflection and adult observation as separate voices, with disagreement retained.

Show exploration breadth as “6 of 15 families tried” only if useful, with an explanation that it is coverage rather than a target or ranking. Within a family, show the chronology and quality of experiences. A current aspiration should be an editable hypothesis, for example “I want to design things that help people,” which can connect to design, engineering, health and community work.

Do not map a school mark directly to an ISCO skill level. Do not infer a fixed career, diagnose traits, produce a universal “career fit percentage”, rank children, or assign a moral score. The portal can explain “This experiment suggests you enjoyed user research; try another project” while keeping its evidence visible.

When circumstances or results change, propose a review: retain the original aspiration, identify the particular requirement affected, distinguish a temporary learning gap from a formal eligibility rule, offer alternative routes, and ask the child/parent/counsellor to select the next plan. A weak exam should never silently delete a career option.

6. Import, provenance and licensing

The official ISCO-08 page links to an Excel structure/definitions file, occupational-title index, skill definitions, CSV structure and Volume I. Use a controlled import from the official file, never scrape a random career website or hand-generate the whole taxonomy.

Recommended tables:

  1. reference_release: publisher, scheme, version, original release, retrieval date, source URL, file checksum, rights notice, licence review state.
  2. occupation_node: release ID, string code preserving leading zeros, hierarchy depth, parent code, canonical title, source locator. Keep translated labels and translation review separately.
  3. occupation_alias: localized job title and disambiguation note, linked to node; identify national variants separately.
  4. exploration_family: our ID, localized label, description, family version.
  5. family_occupation_link: family ID, occupation code, mapping type, scope (whole_group or selected_examples), rationale, editor, reviewed date.
  6. reference_concordance: old and new version codes, relationship and source. Preserve historic records; never silently overwrite a recorded code when ISCO changes.

Validate unique codes, complete parent chains, leading zeros, level counts and sampled task descriptions. Use the official occupational-title index and descriptions to resolve ambiguities; titles alone are insufficient. The 2023 companion guide is the implementation reference. ILO companion guide

Rights require file-level verification. ILO's policy generally applies CC BY 4.0 to qualifying publications/data from 3 May 2023 onward, but older material is not automatically relicensed. Volume I is copyright 2012; a recent upload/crawl date does not change that. Before bundling the full old taxonomy or extensive definitions/translation in a public repository, check the specific dataset terms and obtain clarification/permission if required. Keep source attribution and the prescribed non-endorsement/translation notices where applicable; do not use the ILO emblem as portal branding. This is a release dependency, not a reason to stop designing original activities or showing limited references. ILO rights policy

7. Verification limitations and source anomalies

  • This research verified the published structure, revision notice and selected code references. It did not download, validate or licence-clear a complete 436-unit database.
  • The CSV and Excel download links on the current overview returned a tool fetch error during this run. The overview, official PDF structure and Volume I were readable. Retry and checksum the data files during implementation.
  • The official resource/history page encountered in search included older draft counts (131 minor / 425 unit groups), whereas the current overview and Volume I give 130 / 436. Use the final structure, not a search snippet.
  • The overview's table currently shows skill level 2 for major group 9; the published classification associates elementary occupations with skill level 1. Avoid copying this summary table uncritically; the portal should not expose occupational skill levels as child scores in any event.
  • A regional ILO structure PDF appears to repeat “Police inspectors and detectives” at 3411; use Volume I/current canonical definitions for that record and any import validation. The examples in this design do not rely on 3411.
  • The revision page describes a future timetable and may lag working-group activity. Recheck before production release and when preparing a new reference version.

Hong Kong qualifications investigation — detailed sources & limitations

Hong Kong qualifications, competencies and education pathways

Research checked: 20 October 2026. Primary sources only. This is a product-design reference, not a qualification assessment or individual admissions recommendation. No student's curriculum, grades, residency or career choice has been assumed.

1. What 香港資歷評審 should mean in this product

Several distinct systems must remain separate in the interface and database:

The framework and institutional roles are described by HKQF and HKCAAVQ. The QF webpage notes exceptions for institutions with self-accrediting status, so the product must not imply that every registered university programme receives individual HKCAAVQ accreditation. Product decision: a child's homework, parent observation or portal badge must never be displayed as an awarded QF qualification. Use “參考香港資歷架構的學習範疇” and “校內／家庭觀察”; reserve “QF Level” and “QF credits” for verified recognised qualifications or their published requirements.

It provides an opinion on comparability with a Hong Kong qualification; it is not a daily developmental assessment. Its current guidance excludes, among other things, incomplete qualifications, professional-body memberships and work experience from that service. HKCAAVQ assessment process.

3. SCS and SGC: where the detailed abilities come from

SCS is structured as Units of Competency (UoCs), with code, level, learning volume, scope, performance expectations and assessment requirements. SGC currently covers communication skills, English, Chinese (including Cantonese and Putonghua), information technology and numeracy. VQP connects job roles and needed competencies. HKQF applications.

The current communication SGC PDF is particularly relevant: it contains 26 units at QF levels 2–5 across interpersonal, team, organisational, digital, crisis and creative communication. Example reference codes include 111501L2 for active listening, 111503L3 for emotional understanding and empathy, 111505L4 for constructive feedback, and 111523L3 for storytelling. These are professional reference units; school activities may supply related evidence without satisfying their workplace assessment criteria.

Suggested crosswalk record:

child_skill_id: listening

source_unit: 111501L2
relationship: related_learning_theme
official_assessment_claim: false

This is a many-to-many, human-reviewed relationship. Do not equate an ILO occupation code with a QF level or infer that one hobby qualifies someone for an occupation. Keep the official unit's wording, version and URL separately from an age-appropriate local task.

The ICT SCS page offers detailed branches and topic downloads, including software services, digital media, operations, AI, information security and data science. Its prose totals and displayed subtotals are not fully consistent in the current page. Import individual codes from the chosen source version, validate duplicates and counts, and do not hard-code an unverified total. No verified public bulk API was identified in this research.

4. Qualification levels, credits and recognition limits

The award-title scheme places associate degrees and higher diplomas at QF 4, bachelor's degrees at QF 5, master's degrees at QF 6 and doctorates at QF 7. Certificates and diplomas can span levels; the title alone is not enough to identify the level. Check the exact qualification record. Official award-title scheme.

One QF credit represents ten notional learning hours, including the learning and assessment effort expected for the outcome. This is a programme-volume measure, not an automatic reward for logging ten hours. QF Credit.

資歷學分累積及轉移 (CAT) can support recognition of prior learning and reduce repeated study. Admission and credit transfer remain separate decisions of receiving institutions. A route edge must therefore say “may apply / subject to assessment” unless a specific current articulation agreement establishes the terms. CAT policy and 2024 guidance.

過往資歷認可 (RPL) recognises workplace competencies of industry practitioners through appointed assessment agencies, with qualifications at QF levels 1–4. It is a later-life route, not a mechanism by which parents certify schoolchildren. Recognition of professional qualifications is another distinct assessed route; ordinary membership alone is insufficient. HKQF assessment routes.

QR entries have registration validity periods. The product should retain the period applicable to the learner's intake, plus an immutable verification snapshot; an expired listing must not automatically erase a qualification already earned. The official upload guidance controls registration dates and stresses database accuracy. QR guidance, November 2025. Interpretation of an individual award remains with the provider/authority.

The portal should support parallel routes and switches, not a single ranking from school to an elite university. EDB's current local-study overview includes degrees, subdegrees, vocational/foundation diplomas, in-service training, applied education and repeating Secondary 6. EDB local further study.

HKDSE versus international applications

JUPAS eligibility requires relevant HKDSE results/registration and qualifying identity status, among other conditions. Applicants who do not qualify must check direct/non-JUPAS arrangements with each institution. The official eligibility chart separates local/non-local status and combinations of results. Do not infer status from citizenship or school location alone.

As an example of why curriculum-specific rules matter, HKU's international admissions page distinguishes international and other national qualifications, with separate programme conditions. Its 2026 admissions standards differentiate IB, A-level and SAT/AP combinations and explicitly do not guarantee admission above published thresholds. That document is 2026-intake evidence, not a rule to silently reuse for later admissions. The portal must link current intake pages and display “待更新” when the intended year is not yet published.

Applied learning and career trials

Hong Kong 應用學習 (ApL) is a senior-secondary elective offering practical and theoretical exposure. Its six study areas span creativity, media, business/law, services, applied science and engineering/production. EDB also lists junior and senior taster programmes, with school-mediated application processes. EDB Applied Learning. The 2026 event dates on that page are historical as of this report; they are examples of event types, not currently bookable recommendations.

Suggested portal experiences: observe a demonstration, interview a practitioner, complete a safe small project, then reflect on interest, energy, difficulty and desire to repeat. A single trial is exploration evidence, not a career verdict. Local school/provider accessibility must be checked individually.

Vocational routes

VTC admissions covers degrees, higher diplomas and vocational/foundation qualifications, with differing entry stages. VTC Earn & Learn combines apprenticeship learning and employer participation with scheme support. Treat allowances as dated eligibility-dependent information, not guaranteed family income. A current network engineering example specifically requires relevant bridging courses for progression from its DVE-ELS qualification to higher diploma study; this is why route edges need conditions.

應用教育文憑 (DAE) is distinct from ApL. EDB's FAQ describes a QF 3 qualification for Secondary 6 leavers or adults meeting the age condition, with specified completion, language and articulation requirements. Its recognition comparisons with HKDSE depend on successful completion and, for mathematics inclusion, the Maths Plus course. Do not label it “a complete HKDSE equivalent”. The current page states a recognition period covering 2026/27–2028/29. EDB DAE FAQ.

Applied degrees are a real option within the vocational/professional pathway: the EDB announcement for the 2026/27 SSSDP cohort includes designated applied-degree programmes. Admission, subsidies and professional accreditation must be checked per programme and intake.

6. Implementation requirements derived from the research

These are proposed product rules, not official framework prescriptions:

  1. Keep five separate entities: school result; observed skill evidence; self-reported interest; course requirement; awarded qualification. Never average them into a single child score.
  2. Preserve grades in their original scale, with subject, curriculum, assessment type, marking rubric, date, weight and evidence. Only calculate trends across explicitly comparable assessments.
  3. Each pathway requirement stores: institution, programme, intake year, applicant category, required subjects, grade rule, language rule, application route, deadline/time zone, fees/currency, source URL, verified date, effective dates and reviewer.
  4. Each edge stores whether it is an example, published possibility, formal agreement or confirmed individual offer. A counsellor may override a suggestion but must record why.
  5. Route states: 有興趣 / 正在探索 / 已確認要求 / 正在準備 / 待覆核 / 已申請 / 已獲錄取 / 已轉向. Do not call an aspiration “on track” if prerequisites or current evidence are unknown.
  6. Recalculate a draft scenario after a new report card, subject change, location/budget change, missed deadline or change in interest. Show old/new differences and ask the child/family to adopt the revision; do not silently replace aspirations.
  7. Treat wellbeing, belonging, ethics and service as their own evidence-rich areas. Record context, reflection, repair and next action. Do not issue a moral score, diagnose from behaviour, or equate extroversion with social skill.
  8. Monthly family conversation: what was enjoyable, what became easier, what support is needed, what to try next. Termly pathway review; requirement refresh before a subject choice/application and whenever a cited source changes.
  9. No automatic conversion from logged activity hours to QF credits, from local skill stages to QF levels, from ISCO skill level to school achievement, or from programme entry minimum to admission probability.

7. Source freshness and remaining gaps

  • Research accessed 2026-10-20. The GLD is explicitly the 2018 revision; CAT links 2024 guidance. These are document editions, not research dates.
  • Several HKQF pages show a generic last-revision footer of 7 July 2024 while containing later agency appointment dates or more recent linked files. Store each document's edition and access date independently; do not assume that footer proves the currency of every item.
  • EDB local-study overview shows 16 September 2025 as its last modification. VTC example above says information as of May 2026. Admissions are intake-specific.
  • The current communication SGC PDF was accessed through the live HKQF page; no explicit publication date was found in extracted text. Do not derive publication date from the URL timestamp.
  • No validated complete machine-readable crosswalk exists in this research between ISCO occupations, HK SCS units and the portal's proposed child skills. Such mappings require an editorial review process and must remain labelled as interpretations.
  • No individual QR programme was selected or credential verified. No automatic API, licence for bulk republication, institution integration or real student record was tested.

Child-data design and operating scope

The legal scope depends on the operator, users, location and processing activities. Mainland PIPL Article 72 excludes natural persons processing data for personal/family affairs; that should not automatically be extended to a hosted provider's own processing or a service offered to schools. Where PIPL applies, under-14 personal information is sensitive, and Article 31 requires parent/guardian consent and dedicated handling rules. Articles 38–39 concern overseas provision. PIPL text, especially Articles 28–31, 38–39 and 72.

The 2024 cross-border rules include exemptions and thresholds, but the general low-volume exemption concerns non-sensitive information; it is unsafe to infer that a small child-data service is automatically exempt. Determine the applicable mechanism and any exemption for the actual data flow before sending real records to an overseas backend or AI processor. CAC official explanation of the 2024 cross-border rules.

Hong Kong's PCPD highlights children's limited ability to appreciate over-sharing and recommends age-appropriate protection. Its principles cover collection, accuracy/retention, use, security, openness, access and correction; domestic-purpose exemptions also exist, so organisational deployment should be reviewed separately from a parent's private records. PCPD guidance for services targeting children, PCPD principle overview, PCPD exemptions table.

The following are recommended product requirements, not a statement that each is a statutory rule in every setting:

  • Private by default, no public student search, no class rankings, no advertising SDKs and no automatic public portfolio.
  • Adult owner, student and invited mentor roles with tested access to only their authorised child and evidence. A “child view” toggle in a parent's session is not a security boundary.
  • Invite expiry/revocation, session revocation, password recovery, rate limits and parent MFA. Server enforcement for every read, write and file operation.
  • Explicit child-friendly visibility labels: “my reflection”, “shared with parent”, “shared with mentor”. Do not promise confidentiality inconsistent with actual guardian access and safeguarding duties.
  • Collect grade/year instead of full birth date where sufficient; avoid identity documents, precise live location, diagnoses and sensitive family notes unless a defined necessary function needs them.
  • No “morality”, “loyalty” or overall child-quality score. Ethical growth uses contextual observations and the child's reflection; a disagreement is not a permanent trait label.
  • Allow corrections and alternative interpretations while preserving proportionate audit history. Explain what changed, who changed it and why.
  • Private object buckets, short-lived download access, file type/size controls, malware handling, metadata stripping where appropriate, encrypted transport and backups, and no credentials/student content in logs.
  • Define retention by record purpose, deletion handling including backup expiry, export of both records and evidence files, incident response and restore testing.
  • AI, if added, only drafts summaries or alternatives from approved sources. Give users a review step and provenance; do not automatically send attachments or private reflections to an external model, or use them for training by default.

Delivery sequence and gates

  1. Design validation: synthetic data; validate navigation, Chinese terminology, original grade display, interest exploration and editable route scenarios. Confirm age/curriculum and guardian/student visibility needs.
  2. Private family pilot: choose region and operator; implement identity, memberships, database, private files, consent/notice, records import and export. Pass cross-family denial tests and a backup restore before real uploads.
  3. Evidence and route pilot: review official mappings with an educator/counsellor; test missing prerequisites, changed intake rules, conflicting observations and route rollback. Verify that an exam result never silently closes a future option.
  4. WeChat release: test real devices and networks and resolve account/domain requirements for any WeChat-specific feature. Keep normal web access working if WeChat integration is unavailable.
  5. School/multiple-family expansion: revisit operator responsibilities, contracts, region/transfer rules, support, access segregation, incident response and hosting costs. Add a Mini Program only when its benefit exceeds its extra operation and review work.
