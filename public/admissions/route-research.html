One foundation, several university routes

Research and portal design · checked 30 October 2026 · undergraduate entry

Grade 9 means US Grade 9, not UK Year 9. With Grade 9 in 2026–27, autumn 2030 entry is a planning assumption. Published 2027 dates below are reference-cycle facts, never official 2030 deadlines.

The design decision

Keep destinations open in Grades 9–10, but check subject prerequisites before choosing Grade 10 and Grade 11 courses. Start destination-specific applications preparation in Grade 11. The branch is a change of emphasis, not three separate educations: one appropriate high-school qualification, the same real academic work and evidence, adapted to each institution's requirements.

Subject overlays

STEM needs early mathematics/science sequence checks. Economics can be more mathematically demanding than some business degrees. Humanities need sustained reading, argument and source evaluation. Creative courses need process-based portfolios and auditions. Clinical pathways need science, experience, eligibility and professional checks; standard US medical education follows undergraduate study, unlike direct undergraduate medical programmes in the UK and Hong Kong. Specific institutions and courses determine requirements.

Customization in the portal

Select multiple destinations, degree interest, high-school qualification, US school type, UK timing category, HK course category and provisional applicant status. Record a weekly extra-preparation time budget and family financial constraints. These are planning inputs, not admission predictions. Only relevant branch tasks appear. Existing target universities, saved task states and evidence remain when a branch is hidden; returning to it restores the saved state. Detailed tasks allow editable family dates, owners through custom milestones, notes, completion records and calendar export. Save changes to the private account explicitly.

Review and amendment rules

Review at each term end and before subject-selection deadlines. Compare the student's interest, comparable subject progress, prerequisite gaps, project feedback, workload and affordability. Distinguish school-published grades, internally calculated marks, parent reports, predicted results and external qualifications. Never infer an AP exam score from a school percentage. One lower test mark should prompt diagnosis and support, not automatic exclusion from a country or profession.

At Grade 10's decision point, keep a main route plus an affordable, academically feasible alternative; selective admission remains uncertain and rankings do not make a school safe. When changing routes, record why, what evidence changed, which prerequisites remain available and when to review again. Record plans as plans, not completed achievements. The portal stores review notes and normal record revisions; it does not automatically reassess eligibility or contact schools.

Deadline model

For each actual application track: distinguish opening date, exam registration, exam sitting, school internal handover, application close with timezone, referee/supporting-document close, portfolio/interview, scholarship/aid, decision, acceptance/deposit and final conditions. Family task dates are editable suggestions. Reference-cycle dates can guide future planning, but future-year official dates require fresh verification. The downloadable 29 October comparison workbook is a prior research snapshot; the sources below and the corrected live HKUST rolling label are the latest 30 October update.

The new route does not assume immigration or fee status from citizenship, school location or an identity-card reference. Those eligibility decisions stay with the institution. It also does not assume one English test can be taken years early: check validity and exemption rules in the application cycle.

Next decisions for this family

Confirm whether autumn 2030 is the intended intake. During Grade 9, prioritize reading/writing progress, mathematical understanding, healthy workload and two low-cost interest trials. Before Grade 10 course choices, compare prerequisite maps for two or three possible subject families. The G10 review determines emphasis; it need not permanently close any destination.
